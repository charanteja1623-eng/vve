# Vintage Vibe Events — Product Requirements

## 1. Product Definition

**Product:** Vintage Vibe Events (VVE) main production website  
**Business:** Event organization and event services  
**Market:** Hyderabad, India, with the service area to be confirmed by
the client.

This is a **full production showcase and enquiry website**, not an MVP
or ticketing platform.

The website exists to:

1.  Present the Vintage Vibe Events brand professionally.
2.  Showcase the types of events the company handles.
3.  Showcase the services available.
4.  Demonstrate the quality of previous work.
5.  Explain why a customer should choose VVE.
6.  Make it easy for a potential customer to enquire.
7.  Give the VVE team a maintainable way to manage portfolio/content and
    enquiries.

### Explicit scope boundary

There will be **no payments, checkout, ticket purchasing, customer
accounts, or online transactions** on the website.

The conversion is:

**Visitor → understands VVE → sees work → enquires → VVE team follows
up.**

## 2. Who Are We Making It For?

People looking for an event organizer or event service provider for:

- Weddings
- Birthday Parties
- Surprise Parties
- Baby Showers
- Cradle Ceremonies
- Anniversaries
- Indoor & Outdoor Events
- School & College Events
- New Year Parties
- Corporate Events

These visitors may arrive from Instagram, Google, WhatsApp, referrals,
or the company’s visiting card.

They need to understand quickly:

- What VVE does.
- What events VVE handles.
- What services VVE provides.
- What previous work looks like.
- What benefits VVE offers.
- How to contact the team.
- How to request an enquiry/quote.

These people may already know the company and mainly need:

- Portfolio validation.
- Service details.
- Contact information.
- Location/service-area information.
- A fast WhatsApp/call/enquiry path.

### Internal: VVE team

The team should be able to maintain:

- Portfolio projects.
- Portfolio images.
- Event categories.
- Services.
- Testimonials.
- Basic website content.
- Enquiries.

## 4. Product Goals

### Goal 1 — Showcase

Make the company’s actual event work the strongest proof of capability.

### Goal 2 — Explain

Make event categories and services easy to understand.

### Goal 3 — Differentiate

Explain the practical benefits of using VVE, such as coordinated event
services, customization, design, and execution.

Do not invent claims such as years of experience, number of events, or
customer counts. Use only client-confirmed facts.

### Goal 4 — Convert

Make enquiry actions obvious:

- Enquire Now
- Plan Your Event
- WhatsApp
- Call
- Email
- Contact form

### Goal 5 — Build Trust

Use:

- Real portfolio work.
- Real testimonials.
- Clear service descriptions.
- Real contact information.
- Social proof.
- Consistent branding.

## 5. Brand Direction

The supplied logo and visiting card are the primary brand references.

### Brand characteristics

- Vintage
- Elegant
- Premium
- Traditional Indian
- Warm
- Celebratory
- Refined

### Visual identity

Primary direction:

- Deep burgundy / wine
- Antique gold
- Warm ivory
- Dark plum / charcoal
- Classic serif typography
- Restrained Indian ornamental details

The official VVE logo must remain unchanged.

The website should feel like:

**A modern digital interpretation of a premium Indian event
invitation.**

Avoid:

- Generic wedding templates.
- SaaS aesthetics.
- Gaming aesthetics.
- Nightclub aesthetics.
- Excessive neon.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Overly ornamental layouts that reduce readability.

## 8. Full Production Scope

This is not an MVP with planned feature expansion.

The target is the **complete production showcase website** within the
defined business scope.

“Complete” means:

- All agreed public pages.
- Complete visual design system.
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile experience.
- Full portfolio structure.
- Services and event categories.
- Enquiry workflow.
- Admin/content management where required.
- SEO.
- Accessibility.
- Performance optimization.
- Security.
- Analytics if agreed.
- Legal pages.
- Production deployment.

It does **not** mean adding unrelated product features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Explicitly Out of Scope

Do not build:

- Online payments.
- Checkout.
- Ticket purchasing.
- Event ticket generation.
- Customer accounts.
- E-commerce cart.
- Payment gateway integration.
- Online invoices.
- Subscription system.

A customer can enquire about an event, but the transaction happens
outside the website.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Success Criteria

The site succeeds when a first-time visitor can quickly answer:

1.  Who is Vintage Vibe Events?
2.  What events do they organize?
3.  What services do they provide?
4.  What does their actual work look like?
5.  What benefits do they offer?
6.  Can they handle my type of event?
7.  How do I contact them?
8.  How do I submit an enquiry?

Primary business conversion:

**Qualified enquiry → WhatsApp/phone/email follow-up.**

Secondary outcomes:

- Portfolio engagement.
- Instagram visits.
- Calls.
- WhatsApp conversations.
- Returning/referral visitors.
